## About

I break things first, then build the compliant control that stops the next person doing it. Most of my work sits where attacking, defending and governance hand over to each other.

I am the founder of **ZeroHook**, where I am building a DNS security and compliance auditing platform from scratch. The infrastructure is architected against NIST guidance (**NIST CSF**, **NIST SP 800-30**), and the platform measures DNS and email integrity and audits how it drifts over time. To connect technical engineering with enterprise governance, I am preparing for the **ISO/IEC 27001 Lead Auditor** certification.

| Side | What that means in practice |
| --- | --- |
| Offense | Web, network and Active Directory pentesting, **bug bounty**, CTF, **reverse engineering** |
| Defense | **GRC**, auditing and risk management on **NIST CSF** and **NIST SP 800-30**, **ISO 27001** |
| Build | **DNS security and compliance auditing**, security tooling in **.NET and C#**, full-stack web, ML work in PyTorch and pandas |

Specialties: **web exploitation** (XSS, SQLi, IDOR, CSRF, SSRF), **privilege escalation**, **DNS and email hardening** (SPF, DKIM, DMARC, DNSSEC).

Competitive CTF player since 2023, national and international events. **Top 8% on TryHackMe** at 16. Open to security engineering and GRC roles across Europe, remote or on-site.
